    06 / UNIT ECONOMICS
    
            
    Does voice ordering pay for itself?
    
            
    Cost per order for booking, delivery follow-up and collections, done by a telecaller, a field rep, or a voice agent. The point of the model: copying today's long calls onto AI saves about a third at best, and loses to cheaper telecallers. Redesigning the calls to be short is where the real saving comes from.
